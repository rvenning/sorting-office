❓ How to Play

  1. A parcel arrives on the belt. The yellow banner says how to sort it.
  2. Drag it onto a postbox — or just tap the box you want.
  3. Wrong box? It hops back out and you try again. Nothing is lost.
  4. Get them right first time to earn stars. Five in a row doubles your points.
  5. In the Reading Room, some parcels say a word no box has — those go in the returns bin.
  6. When the bell rings 🔔 the rule changes and the boxes swap labels. Look again!
